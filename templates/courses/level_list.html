About Cambridge YLE Tests

Cambridge Young Learners English tests are designed for children in primary and lower-secondary school. The tests use topics and situations that children are familiar with, making the experience enjoyable.

  • Starters: First step in a child's English language learning journey (Pre A1)
  • Movers: Next step after Starters, building confidence (A1)
  • Flyers: Highest level of YLE tests, prepares for A2 Key (A2)
